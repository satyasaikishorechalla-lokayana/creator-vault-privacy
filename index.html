Creator Vault - Privacy Policy

Publisher: Lokayana Space App: Creator Vault (package: space.lokayana.creatorvault) Effective date: 24 November 2026 (ML Kit scanning metrics disclosed) Contact: lokayana.spacetech@zohomail.in

The short version

Creator Vault is an offline app. Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. There is no account, no server, and no advertising. We do not collect, see, or store any of your data. Receipt scanning runs on your phone; its Google ML Kit library sends Google only technical performance metrics (see Receipt scanning below).

What the app stores on your device

  • Payment entries you record: client or consultancy name, amounts, payment and invoice dates, payment status, PAN and GST numbers you choose to type in, and your own notes.
  • Planner items and reminder dates you set (content timelines, expected payment dates, TDS review reminders).
  • Invoice details you create. Invoice PDFs are generated on your phone only when you choose to share them; the app does not keep copies of the PDFs.
  • Lock details: your PIN, biometric preference, and one-time recovery code, stored only on your phone so the app can verify them when you unlock or reset.
  • App settings such as your reminder choices and backup preference.
  • Receipt images attached in older versions of the app: kept encrypted on your device until you delete those payments. New receipt scans are never stored.

Receipt scanning (data only)

When you capture or upload a receipt photo or PDF, the app reads the text on it, on your device, to prefill fields such as the amount, date, TDS, and net received. It fills only from amounts printed on the receipt and never guesses.

To read a receipt, the app makes a temporary working copy on your device and deletes that copy right after reading. Your original file stays wherever you keep it, for example your gallery or Downloads; the app never deletes your own files. New receipt scans are not saved in the app, are not uploaded anywhere, and are not included in backups. Only the extracted details you confirm are kept.

Receipts you attached in older versions of the app (before November 2026) stay encrypted on your device and inside your encrypted backups until you delete those payments. You can share or remove them from the payment entry.

Receipt scanning runs on your device using Google's ML Kit. Your receipt images and text never leave your phone. The ML Kit library itself sends Google technical metrics about how the scanning feature performs: device model and OS version, app version, an identifier for each app install, performance timings, and feature events (such as feature starts and detections). This data is encrypted in transit, is used by Google for diagnostics and analytics, and is not shared with third parties. Google's own description: developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

What we do not collect

  • No receipt, ledger, or other personal content is sent off your device by the app. The receipt-scanning library sends Google only the technical metrics described above.
  • No advertising, crash-tracking SDKs, or third-party trackers. The only analytics are the ML Kit scanning-library metrics described above, which go to Google and contain no receipt or ledger content.
  • No account or sign-up: we never ask for your name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • No access to your PAN, GST, client, or payment data by the developer, at any point.

Security

Your data is stored in an encrypted database on your phone. You lock the app with a PIN and can also use your phone's biometric unlock (fingerprint or face), which is handled by Android itself; the app never sees or stores your biometric data.

When you set your PIN, the app shows you a one-time recovery code to write down and keep somewhere safe outside the app. If you forget your PIN, you can enter that code on the lock screen to set a new PIN; your records stay untouched on your phone. Each recovery code works once, and repeated wrong PIN or recovery-code attempts temporarily lock the app.

Optional backup

Backup is off by default. If you turn it on, the app creates an encrypted backup file that you choose where to keep (for example your own cloud storage or device). The backup is encrypted on your phone before it leaves the app. We cannot read it and we do not hold a copy. If you lose both your phone and your backups, your data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us. The app can remind you monthly to take a backup if you enable it.

What leaves your phone, and when

Only what you deliberately share, through your own apps:

  • Invoice PDFs you share with clients (for example over email or WhatsApp).
  • Payment reminder messages: the app can open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message, and you review and send it yourself.
  • Summaries you export for your chartered accountant.

None of this passes through any server of ours.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to scan receipts. Used only while you scan.
  • Photos / files: to pick a receipt image or PDF you choose. Used only for that pick; the app's temporary copy of it is deleted after reading, and your original file is left untouched.
  • Notifications: for reminders you set (payments due, planner dates, TDS reviews, monthly backup).
  • Biometric: to unlock the app if you enable it. Handled entirely by Android.

Children

Creator Vault is a finance record-keeping tool for working adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Any backups you made are files you own; delete them wherever you kept them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same link with a new effective date. The app will remain offline-first: your receipts and ledger stay on your device, and any future feature that moves your content off your device will be opt-in and clearly explained first.
